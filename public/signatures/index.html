  1. Open your signature page. Wait for the logo to load.
  2. Press Ctrl+A, then Ctrl+C (Command+A / Command+C on Mac).
  3. In Outlook, open the signature editor and paste with Ctrl+V. Settings differ between classic Outlook, new Outlook and Outlook on the web.
  4. Save it and assign it to the appropriate sending account.
  5. Send a test email, then inspect the received message, a reply and a forward. Check the logo, spacing, contact links and James’s LinkedIn link.

The signature uses a public HTTPS PNG image. A recipient’s email application may still require them to allow external pictures. Preview page access may require a Vercel sign-in; the image itself does not.

These are HTML signatures. Paste the rendered signature, not the webpage address or HTML source.
